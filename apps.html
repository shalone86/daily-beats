Apps

Free music toys to play with. They run in your browser, with no sign-up, no ads, and nothing tracked. On Android you can also install them as an app.

Boba Beats

A beat maker made of bubbles. Tap out drums, bass, chords and melodies (every bubble is in tune), loop them, build a song, and export it.

Play Android app

Deep Drift

A dark, quiet sea of sleepy jellyfish that sing when you touch them. Tap a few, let the notes drift and loop, and set the sleep timer.

Play Android app

Pop Pads

A fizzy little drum machine. Seven soda-flavored kits, from 808 and 909 to lo-fi and a toy kit made of soda sounds. Tap, roll and jumble the pads, chain patterns, and send your beat to Boba Beats.

Play Android app

Cozy Chords

A warm little chord harp. Tap a chord, swipe the strings to strum it, add a gentle beat with a bass that follows along, and record a song to take into Logic or Boba Beats.

Play

The Android apps install from the file ("sideloading"): your phone will ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time.
